The recordings have been trimmed (up to 90 seconds), lightly high-pass filtered,
balanced in loudness, crossfaded at the loop boundary, and encoded as stereo MP3.
Credits and license links are also available in the audio dock.

The default music comes from the project owner's supplied music.mp4 compilation.
Titles, artists, and song boundaries follow the owner's track list in
src/lib/audio-library.json. The original AAC stream is split into 24 M4A tracks
without re-encoding. The ambience licenses above apply only to those recordings.
